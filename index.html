Machine learning for pharmacy inventory

Know which medicines will run out before they do.

This project predicts the chance that a product stocks out before its next delivery arrives, so pharmacists can reorder the right items first and keep essential medicines on the shelf.

From sales history to a reorder decision

The project follows the path a pharmacy analyst would take by hand, then automates it and measures how well it works.

  1. Collect

    Daily dispensing and stock records per product, supplier lead times and delivery history.

  2. Engineer features

    Rolling demand averages, variability, days of cover, weekday and seasonal effects, recent stockouts.

  3. Predict

    A classifier estimates the probability of stocking out within the next lead time window for each product.

  4. Decide

    Products are ranked by risk with a plain-language reason, so the order list is ready each morning.

What goes into the model

Planned inputs and tooling. Each choice favors methods that pharmacy staff can inspect and question.

Signals

  • Rolling 7, 14 and 28 day demand
  • Demand variability per product
  • Days of cover against lead time
  • Weekday and seasonal patterns
  • Supplier delay history

Evaluation

  • Time-based train and test split, never random
  • Precision and recall at the top of the ranked list
  • Calibration of predicted probabilities
  • Comparison against the formula baseline
